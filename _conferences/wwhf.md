The Wild West Hackin' Fest was started in Deadwood, South Dakota, but has recently expanded to include Denver in their "Mile High" edition of WWHF!

---

## Deadwood, SD
Deadwood, South Dakota is a historic town in the Black Hills known for its Old West history, preserved downtown, casinos, museums, and nearby outdoor access. The perfect setting for Wild West Hackin' Fest!

## Deadwood GF Eats

1. Mavericks Steak & Cocktails - Large amount of gluten-free options designed by the chef who has Celiac.
2. Legends Steakhouse - Another steakhouse, while a slightly smaller menu of options for gluten-free food. 
3. Jersey Mike's Subs - A fast food chain, however, the chain does have gluten-free bread for subs, and they will clean the grill and utensils upon stating that you have Celiac/gluten allergy.



## Denver 
Denver is the Mile High city, and while you might be struggling for oxygen, you will certainly not be struggling for good gluten free eats!

## Denver GF Eats
{: .fs-2}

1. **Moore Bread** - Two locations, one near downtown, and one in Lakewood. 100% gluten free, they serve breakfast and lunch sandwiches, as well as donuts, carrot cake, cannoli, and other assortments of desserts. They also have a series of delicious coffee and matcha drinks. 
2. Brutal Poodle - Gluten free mac and cheese. What else can I say?
3. **Holidaily** - Completely gluten free beer. Not gluten removed, gluten free. Best GF beer you'll have.
4. LaMai Thai - Wonderful Thai restaurant with gluten-free curry and pad thai, with other menu items that can be altered to be gluten-free.
5. Sushi Den/Izakaya Den - Most of the menu is already gluten free. The items that are not are prepared separately. Some items can be made gluten free if they are not already. 
6. Woody's Wood-Fired Pizza - Gluten free pizza, chicken wings, and fried cauliflour. Gluten free items are prepared separately, but it is a shared kitchen. 
7. 5280 Burger - Dedicated friers, so all the fries are gluten free. Burgers are great with either gluten free bun or lettuce wrap.  
8. Arepas House - Not dedicated gluten free, but only one item on their menu contains gluten, and is kept completely separate from everything else on the menu. 
9. Acova - Entire menu is gluten free, minus 2-3 menu items. Including gluten free fish and chips! 
10. **Quiero Arepas** - 100% gluten-free restaurant with multiple locations across Denver and Boulder. 
11. **Teocalli Cocina** - Fully gluten-free mexican restaurant. Great if you have a craving for tacos!
12. **Rivers and Roads Coffee** - Despite the name, Rivers and Road is not just a coffee shop. While they do offer delicious coffee and mactha drinks, they also offer a series of savory sandwiches and a rotating menu of dessert items. 


(**Dedicated Gluten Free**, Not Dedicated Gluten Free)
