[Black Hat USA](https://www.blackhat.com/) is one of the best-known professional cybersecurity events in the world. It takes place in Las Vegas during Hacker Summer Camp, alongside BSides Las Vegas and DEF CON, and brings together security researchers, defenders, executives, vendors, and practitioners from across the industry.

Black Hat has a more structured and industry-focused atmosphere than DEF CON. The event begins with multi-day, hands-on Trainings covering a wide range of offensive and defensive security topics. These are followed by the main conference, where researchers present peer-reviewed Briefings on newly discovered vulnerabilities, emerging threats, defensive techniques, and other developments in information security.

There is much more to explore beyond the scheduled talks. Arsenal gives researchers and developers a place to demonstrate open-source security tools, while the Business Hall includes vendor exhibits, community programs, sponsored sessions, and networking opportunities. Black Hat also hosts keynotes, specialized summits, and activities for first-time attendees. Pass types provide access to different parts of the event, so review the current [registration options](https://www.blackhat.com/us-26/registration.html) and schedule before making plans.

## Gluten-Free Food at Black Hat

Black Hat USA is currently held at the Mandalay Bay Convention Center. Some conference passes include continental breakfast, breaks, and lunch on the main Briefings days, but meal access depends on the pass type and may change from year to year.

If you have celiac disease, a wheat allergy, or need to avoid cross-contact, contact the event organizers before the conference to ask about current accommodations. When food is served, confirm ingredients and preparation practices with the catering staff rather than relying only on a menu label. Conference days can be long, so carrying a few safe snacks is also a good backup plan.

Mandalay Bay and the surrounding Las Vegas Strip have additional dining options. See the [Hacker Summer Camp guide]({{ '/conferences/hackersummercamp/' | relative_url }}) for the collective's nearby gluten-free recommendations.
